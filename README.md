# SAFE CALL: AI Voice Clone Detection and Intelligent Scam Response Guidance

## Project Overview

SAFE CALL is an AI-based project aimed at detecting AI-generated or cloned voices and identifying potential scams during voice-based communication.
The proposed system combines audio-based deepfake detection with speech transcription and scam-related language analysis to assess potential risks and provide intelligent safety guidance to users.

## Objectives

- Detect real and AI-generated or cloned voices.
- Convert speech into text for further analysis.
- Identify potential scam indicators, such as impersonation, urgency, threats, and requests for financial or confidential information.
- Combine detection results to assess risk levels.
- Provide explainable safety recommendations to help users respond to potentially suspicious calls.

## ⚙️ Proposed Methodology

The proposed workflow consists of the following stages:

1. **Audio Preprocessing:** Prepare audio recordings for analysis.
2. **Voice Deepfake Detection:** Analyze audio to distinguish genuine speech from AI-generated or cloned voices.
3. **Speech-to-Text Conversion:** Transcribe speech into text.
4. **Scam Detection:** Analyze transcripts for suspicious language and scam-related indicators.
5. **Risk Assessment:** Combine relevant outputs to estimate the potential risk.
6. **Safety Guidance:** Generate appropriate recommendations based on the assessed risk.

## 🛠️ Proposed Technologies

The final technology stack will be confirmed during implementation.

- **Programming Language:** Python
- **Deep Learning:** PyTorch
- **Audio Processing:** Audio preprocessing libraries
- **Voice Analysis:** Pretrained speech/audio models
- **Speech Transcription:** Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)
- **Text Analysis:** NLP and text classification models
